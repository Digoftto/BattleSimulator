# BS Combat

## Primary SSoT

Read in this order as needed:

1. `COMBAT_RULES.md`
2. `COMBAT_CORE.md`
3. `ABILITIES.md`
4. `CARD_CATALOG.md` for card-specific data
5. `AFFINITY.md` for affinity behavior

Do not load unrelated economy/city documents unless the combat rule explicitly depends on them.

## Critical rules

- Combat maximum is 64 turns.
- Attacks/actions follow the documented turn sequence.
- Target snapshots are not silently replaced by new targets.
- Secondary attacks/effects generated by an ability are consequences of the primary action unless the ability explicitly defines another action.
- Death resolution occurs according to the canonical combat sequence.
- A War Machine/Balista must begin at position 9.
- Starting at position 9 does NOT make a War Machine immobile; after combat begins it follows the normal movement rule.
- Affinity is evaluated between turns. Active bonuses remain frozen for the entire current turn.
- Deaths, summons, or other composition changes during a turn do not retroactively change active Affinity bonuses.

## Implementation rule

Do not encode combat behavior from a card description if the behavior is already defined by `COMBAT_RULES.md` or `ABILITIES.md`.
